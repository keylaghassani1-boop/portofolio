AI mobile app concept | Student creativity proposal | Group project

Thumb: AI-Based Mobile App to Detect Hoaxes and Digital Scams for Older Adults

An AI app that helps older adults spot hoaxes and online scams.

  • Mobile app
  • AI text, image & link analysis
  • Proposed design
Problem
Older adults with low digital literacy are vulnerable to hoaxes and scams on WhatsApp, Facebook, and YouTube.
Solution
Users send a screenshot or link and AI explains if it is risky. Features: HoaxShield, TrustCheck (link safety), and ScamSense (literacy quizzes).
My contribution
Theoretical study, implementation plan, educational content, and progress report.
